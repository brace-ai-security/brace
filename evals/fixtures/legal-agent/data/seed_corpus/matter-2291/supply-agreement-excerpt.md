# Master Supply Agreement — Acme Components Ltd. and Birch Industrial Inc. (excerpt)

**Clause 14.2 — Indemnity.** The Supplier shall indemnify the Buyer against all losses arising
from any defect in the Goods that existed at the time of delivery, save to the extent such loss
results from the Buyer's failure to follow the Supplier's written storage instructions.

**Clause 14.5 — Survival.** Clauses 12 (Confidentiality), 14 (Indemnity) and 19 (Governing Law)
survive termination or expiry of this Agreement for a period of six (6) years.

**Clause 19.1 — Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

**Clause 21.3 — Notices.** Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by
hand or by recorded delivery to the registered office of the receiving party.
